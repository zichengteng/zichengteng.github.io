PanoTac: A Dense Three-Axis Tactile Sensor for Robust Touch-Centric Manipulation
Binglin (Andre) Wang†, Xiaole (Alex) Jiang†, Zicheng Teng, Xinqi Liu, Yunzhu Li, Lerrel Pinto, Zichen Jeff Cui, Binghao Huang († Equal contribution)
arXiv preprint, 2026
project page | arXiv

PanoTac is a low-cost, compliant fingertip sensor for dense three-axis tactile sensing. Across test-tube preparation, grain scooping, and salt pouring, visuo-tactile policies outperform vision-only baselines by an average of 46.7 percentage points.
